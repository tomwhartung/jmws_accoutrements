
Who's Better: Michael Bay or Andrei Tarkovsky?

I have a dream, that one day I will be chatting with someone, a neighbor or someone I meet at a local kava bar or the like, and I learn that they saw the Sex Pistols back in the 1970s, that time when they toured Britain before Sid Vicious joined and Glen Matlock was still on bass.

In my dream, seeing this band instantly changed their life, and they - like so many other bands, such as X-Ray Spex, Joy Division, and The Smiths - wanted to do something similarly totally original and equally disruptive.

But ooops, they got hit by a chair during the show and wound up in a coma!  Rats!

Subsequently, their parents relocated to Denver, Colorado - which coincidentally is where I live - bringing their comatose child along with them, and lo and behold, they miraculously woke up, and met me one fine day!

Don't you agree that is quite a dream?  But wait, there's more!

This child, whose name is Alex let's say, quickly realizes that we now have an internet connecting everyone on the planet, and a violent mob of internet trolls has taken control of America and is trying to turn the clock back to a time before The Enlightenment.
Imagine how Alex might feel about this, having lost their dream of following in the Sex Pistols' footsteps!

Fortunately, Alex parents were not rich but comfortable, and thus left their child with a modest inheritance.

In my dream, Alex realizes that there are so many musicians around that following in the Pistols' footsteps is just not realistic, because they'd just be drowned out by all the competition.
However, Alex can also see that, thanks to smart phones, youtube, and Open Source Software, someone who wanted to disrupt things could use a modest amount of capital to create a movie!

Of course, Alex is now someone with a teenager's brain stuck in a 65-year-old's body.
